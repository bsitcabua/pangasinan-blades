Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pangasinan Blades handles information in connection with the Pangasinan Blades Email Assistant, including information accessed through Google APIs.

Effective date: August 25, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Pangasinan Blades Email Assistant and related backend services used by authorized Pangasinan Blades administrators to manage customer email inquiries. It covers data received directly from customers and data accessed through an authorized Google account.

2. Information we may process

  • customer names, email addresses, contact details, shipping or location information, and other details customers include in their messages;
  • email subjects, message bodies, timestamps, sender/recipient information, and Gmail message or thread identifiers;
  • draft and sent-message information necessary to manage replies;
  • Google account information necessary to identify and maintain the authorized Gmail connection; and
  • OAuth credentials or tokens necessary for the backend to maintain authorized access to Google APIs.

3. How we use Google user data

  • retrieving customer inquiries from the connected Gmail account;
  • associating related messages with the correct Gmail conversation;
  • classifying inquiries and extracting details needed to prepare a response;
  • generating suggested reply wording using verified Pangasinan Blades business data;
  • creating or updating Gmail drafts;
  • surfacing cases that require human review; and
  • sending a message through Gmail when an authorized administrator explicitly initiates or approves the send action.

Pangasinan Blades does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell Google user data, and does not use Gmail user data to train generalized AI models.

4. AI-assisted processing

The Email Assistant may send selected customer inquiry content and relevant business context to an AI service to classify inquiries, extract structured request details, or prepare draft wording. AI-generated output is subject to backend validation and, where required, human review. Authoritative product information and pricing remain controlled by Pangasinan Blades backend data and business rules.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may disclose or process information through service providers only as necessary to operate the Email Assistant, such as Google services used for Gmail and OAuth, hosting infrastructure, database services, and AI-processing services. These providers process data for the purpose of delivering their respective services. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect rights, security, or the integrity of the service.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Pangasinan Blades' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

7. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect application data. OAuth credentials and tokens are handled by backend systems and are not intended to be exposed to the public website or client-side application code. Access to administrative email-management functions is limited to authorized users.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Email Assistant, maintain customer conversation history, support audit and security needs, comply with legal or business recordkeeping obligations, and resolve disputes. Data may be deleted, anonymized, or retained for a longer period where required or permitted by applicable law.

9. Revoking Google access

The owner of the connected Google account may revoke the application's Google access through Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access by the application, but does not automatically delete information that was lawfully stored before revocation where retention remains necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Google account connection permissions can generally be managed at Google Account connections.

10. Requests and deletion

To ask about personal information, request correction or deletion, or ask that Pangasinan Blades remove stored Google-connected data where applicable, contact inquire@pangasinanblades.com. We may need to verify the requester's identity before acting on a request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published. If a material change affects how Google user data is used, appropriate notice and consent will be provided where required.
