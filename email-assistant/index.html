Pangasinan Blades Email Assistant

The Pangasinan Blades Email Assistant is an internal customer-email management system that helps authorized Pangasinan Blades administrators receive, organize, review, draft, and respond to customer inquiries using Gmail.

Public information page for the Pangasinan Blades Google OAuth application.

Customer Email Management

Organizes incoming customer inquiries and related conversation history so authorized administrators can review them in one workflow.

Draft Assistance

Uses backend business data and AI-assisted wording to prepare suggested replies and quotations for administrator review.

Human Approval

Supports human-review workflows for cases that need manual decisions. Automated quotation generation is designed to stop at a draft until an authorized administrator sends a reply.

Why Google access is requested

The application connects to an authorized Gmail account so the Pangasinan Blades backend can perform the email-management functions the administrator has enabled. Depending on the Google permissions granted, this may include reading message content and metadata needed to process customer inquiries, associating messages with Gmail threads, creating or updating Gmail drafts, and sending a reply when an authorized administrator explicitly chooses to send it.

How Google user data is used

  • identifying and retrieving customer inquiries received by the connected Gmail account;
  • preserving message and thread context for customer conversations;
  • analyzing inquiry content to classify requests and extract relevant customer-request details;
  • preparing draft responses based on authoritative Pangasinan Blades product and business data;
  • flagging uncertain or sensitive cases for human review;
  • creating or updating Gmail drafts; and
  • sending replies only when the applicable workflow and an authorized administrator permit sending.

AI-assisted processing

Selected customer inquiry content may be processed by an AI service to classify inquiries and prepare draft wording. Pangasinan Blades uses this processing only to provide and improve the email-management functionality described on this page. Pangasinan Blades does not use Gmail user data to train generalized AI models.

Control and transparency

The connected Google account remains under the control of its account owner. Google permissions can be revoked from Google Account settings. If access is revoked, the Email Assistant may stop receiving or processing Gmail data until the account is reconnected by an authorized administrator.
